## 5. Look and craft standard
Look: <GHIBLI or CEL, linework, texture, haze, grade>. Motion: slow, confident, eased; hard cuts only on beats.
Zero tolerance: z-fighting, flicker, popping, clipping, floating, sliding feet, props hovering beside the hand that
holds them, camera inside geometry, faceless characters, text over text, UI covering action, black or washed frames
except intentional dips, shader errors, off-palette colours, cut-off labels, jitter.
